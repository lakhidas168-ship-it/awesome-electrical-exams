# UPSC ESE (EE) — official papers list

Source of truth: <https://upsc.gov.in> (opened 2026-09-28).

## How to find a paper

1. Open <https://upsc.gov.in>.
2. Go to "Previous Question Papers" (under Examinations).
3. Select "Engineering Services Examination" and the year.
4. Download the Electrical Engineering papers.

Direct PDF URLs on upsc.gov.in change per year, so they are not hard-coded
here — always navigate from the site itself.
